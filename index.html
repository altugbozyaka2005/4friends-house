The House

A Georgian home with a multilingual welcome

Behind the green door on Ferry Road is a lovingly restored Georgian townhouse with ornate cornicing, a sweeping original staircase and big sash windows that pour light into every room.

Your hosts are on hand with tips for dinner and walks nearby. Coffee, tea, biscuits and fruit wait in the lounge. Leith's harbour-front restaurants, the Water of Leith Walkway and the Royal Botanic Garden are all close by — and Princes Street is one bus away.

Good to know

Questions, answered

What are check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 15:00 and check-out is by 11:00. Arriving late? Just let us know — we send clear self check-in instructions before you arrive, and luggage storage is available.

Is breakfast included?

A simple self-serve breakfast of toast, cereal and fruit is available, along with coffee, tea and biscuits in the lounge and a guest fridge.

Is there parking?

Free public and on-street parking is available nearby on and around Ferry Road.

Is the house accessible?

Rooms are on the first and second floors of a Georgian townhouse with a traditional staircase and no lift. Please get in touch if stairs are a concern and we'll suggest the best room.

Can I bring children?

Yes. Most double, king and triple rooms sleep two adults plus one child, cots are available on request, and the Superior King can take an extra folding bed for £30. Board games, books and DVDs for children are in the lounge.

What is the cancellation policy?

Choose a flexible direct rate, or save 10% with a non-refundable rate paid in full at booking. The exact terms for your dates are shown before you pay.

Is the house non-smoking?

Yes, the whole property is non-smoking.
